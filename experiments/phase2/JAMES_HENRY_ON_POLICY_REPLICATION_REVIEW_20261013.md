## Concrete sequential mechanism, distinct from our R48

[chain_port_refit.py](https://github.com/jamesrahenry/arc-whitebox-replication/blob/8d6cd4f4f6ec6672914bc002350f6a40da92ca84/moment_chain/chain_port_refit.py) confirms sequential fitting, not merely a narrative DAgger label. It initializes each TRAINnetwork state to(0,I). At layerl, the raw Gaussian closure advances the state already corrected by all frozen layers<l, derives8mean/6variance/2offdiagonal features, fits against true postmean/covariance, then applies the learned correction to TRAINstates. The next layer therefore sees the model's own corrected rollout rather than truth resets. Held nets call `run_corrected` with the frozen per-layer coefficients and no truth entering state. This is one sequential supervised pass, DAgger-style in the sense of matching rollout state, not full iterative dataset aggregation with a theoretical DAgger guarantee.

Cumulant features are supplied by one runtime MC4096probe through all32layers: preκ3, preκ4, and full pairfield E[zc_i² zc_j]. The state holds full covariance. Their correction updates covariance offdiagonals with two symmetric matrix features as well as mean/variance; our R48updates four marginal diagonals with original approximate cross/source ledger frozen. We cannot directly reuse their pair-correction fit without offdiagonal truth or an independently measured projected covariance target.

R48TRAINstates before each localtarget were advanced with MCoracle post4resets in priorlayers. It learned a teacher-forced local residual. Fresh learned rolloutR49 was flat/worse(+.2504%). R38learned all per-layer heads against a fixed parent baseline trajectory, also not on-policy sequential corrected fitting. Thus neither experiment validates or rejects Henry's sequential self-rollout mechanism. On-policy collection can address our demonstrated exposurebias without changing features, source representations, ridge or candidate arithmetic, making it the cleanest next single-mechanism test.

## Recommended priority for our project

Prioritize a true sequential on-policy TRAINpilot before more static graphfeature capacity. Keep R48's fixed26features, per-layerlinear ridge.01, σ-normalizedfourchannels, floors, layer0/final15skip and frozen H18source ledger. At each layer1..14, advance each TRAINnetwork using already fitted heads<l; collect currentownpre/postfeatures, fit this one head against offlineMCpostlabels, apply learned head rather than truth. For each networkCVfold, fit allprefixheads only on foldTRAIN18, then evaluate held6with no fitting/truthstate resets; reusing globally fittedprefix heads on foldheldnets would leaklabels. Fresh unseen8must remain untouched until allheads/freeze chosen; score actualfulllearnedrollout finalmean, not localCVκ3/4alone. Actualruntimecandidate allfnp remains distinct from offlinefitting/capture.

This is a conceptualmechanism recommendation, not a forecast of10×. Its cost is substantial because H18state includes sourcehistory and cannot be reduced to mean/cov2arrays; immutable prefixcheckpoints or deterministicrestart-capture must retain exactsourceledger/pruning/perm. A shortTRAINpilot can test trajectorycollection correctness and localresidualdirection before committing all24×14. Graph6can remain a separate later26vs32controlled feature comparison; it should not be combined with on-policy at the first test. R51currentlycapturinggraphcontext may finish for futureanalysis, but no noisy/staticteacherfitgain should displace this cleaner exposurebiasexperiment. No cloud started by this review.
